# Artwork credits

The painted flowers in these designs come from public-domain botanical illustrations (CC0 or Public Domain Mark),
found through Openverse. Most are 18th–19th century plates, e.g. by Pierre-Joseph Redouté, and watercolours by
Mary Vaux Walcott (Smithsonian, CC0). Each was cleaned of its old paper, captions and stamps, cut out, and
arranged on new paper for INVTRA. No attribution is required for these works; it is listed here as good practice.
